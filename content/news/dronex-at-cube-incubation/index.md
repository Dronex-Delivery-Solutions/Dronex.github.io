On March 13, our founder Amir Salar participated in the Cube Incubation “Gece Küpü” coding workshop, where developers and entrepreneurs came together to build, share, and present their ideas.

During the event, Amir delivered a session on concurrency and parallelism in modern software systems, with a focus on how reliable autonomous technologies are built in practice. Using Python examples, he covered multiprocessing, multithreading, and asyncio to show how scalable and responsive systems can be designed.

He also showcased a real-world drone communication example using MAVSDK and asyncio, highlighting how autonomous aerial systems can process data streams efficiently and reliably in mission-critical environments.

You can view the sample repository here: https://github.com/Roboticistprogrammer/Workshop.git

At DroneX, we continue to invest in engineering development and community knowledge sharing to support the future of autonomous systems and robotics.

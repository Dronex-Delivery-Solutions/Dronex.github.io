DroneX builds digital twin workflows for industrial, logistics, and robotics teams that need to test automation decisions before they affect live operations. A digital twin connects facility geometry, operational rules, robot behavior, and live data into a model that can support safer deployment and better planning.

![Warehouse digital twin concept](/media/digital_twin.png)

## Operational Focus

- **Facility modeling:** Capture aisles, racks, safety zones, staging areas, and flight or driving corridors.
- **Autonomy validation:** Test routes, mission plans, coverage logic, and edge cases before hardware is deployed.
- **Operational analysis:** Compare simulated scenarios against real telemetry to identify bottlenecks and improve throughput.
- **Safety planning:** Evaluate no-go zones, human-robot interaction areas, emergency paths, and risk-sensitive workflows.

## Where It Fits

Digital twin services are useful during feasibility studies, pilot deployments, and full automation rollouts. They help teams answer practical questions early: where should robots operate, how many missions can run safely, and which workflow changes create measurable value.

## Delivery Scope

DroneX can support digital twin setup, data model design, simulation workflows, robotics integration, dashboard development, and continuous calibration from live operational data.
